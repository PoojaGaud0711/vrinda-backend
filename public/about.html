From our pharmacist

Wellness Tips

Store medicines right

Most tablets want a cool, dry cabinet — not the fridge, not the bathroom. Heat and humidity degrade them faster than expiry dates do.

Finish the course

Antibiotics feel like they've worked in 3 days — but stopping early breeds resistance. Always complete the prescribed course.

Hydration is medicine

Headaches, fatigue and dull skin often start as dehydration. 2–3 litres a day does more than most supplements.
