Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 6 PM are delivered the same day across Mumbai. After 6 PM, delivery arrives the next morning. Prescription verification may add a short delay for Rx orders.

Do I need a prescription?

Only for prescription (Rx) medicines — marked with a red Rx badge on the product. OTC products, wellness items, snacks and daily essentials don't need one. You can upload your prescription here and we'll handle the rest.

Are the medicines genuine?

100%. We're a government-certified licensed pharmacy and source every product directly from licensed distributors. Cold-chain items like insulin are shipped in proper temperature-controlled packaging.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery is live now. UPI and card payments are coming very soon.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

Reach out within 48 hours — we replace damaged or incorrect items free of charge, no questions asked. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll sort it out.

Can I return medicines?

By law, medicines can't be resold once delivered — but damaged, expired or wrong items are always replaced. Non-medicine items (snacks, beauty) follow standard return rules.
